---
title: Share product documents with trade customers
tags: trade, downloads, documents, images, zip, datasheets
---

**Trade Downloads** gives signed-in trade customers a page listing your shop's product documents and images, grouped by category. A one-click ZIP downloads all of a product's images.

It has no settings of its own. It uses the documents and images already attached to your products in the shop, so keep those up to date and customers see the change.

It needs the **Trade Accounts** service and the shop. Your site's design decides where the page appears.
